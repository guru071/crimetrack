Getting Started

C.A.S.E is an independent police records management system designed for field teams and law enforcement agencies.

🎯 Key Features:
  • Local-first criminal record management
  • Firebase-based officer authentication
  • Encrypted peer-to-peer operations room
  • Biometric security (fingerprint/face lock)
  • Face recognition search
  • Google Sheets integration (optional)
  • Offline-capable with sync
  • Audit logging for compliance

System Requirements

  • Device: Android phone, tablet, or desktop browser
  • Connection: Internet for login & sync (offline operation supported)
  • Storage: Minimum 100MB free space
  • Permissions: Camera (face recognition), microphone (voice input), biometric sensors

Login & Authentication

Creating an Account

  1. Open the app and click Sign Up
  2. Enter your email address and password
  3. Create your officer profile (name, station, photo)
  4. Check your email for verification link
  5. Verify your account and log in
⚠️ Email Verification: Check your inbox for a Firebase verification email. If not received, click "Resend Verification" in the login screen.

App Lock - Biometric Security

When you resume the app after minimizing it, you will be prompted for biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition). This ensures your record data is protected.

  • First time: Allow biometric permissions when prompted
  • Subsequent opens: Unlock with your biometric
  • Failing biometric check will log you out for security
💡 Tip: Register your primary fingerprint first. Test biometric on your device settings before using the app.

Managing Criminal Records

Adding a New Record

  1. Click +Add Record button
  2. Fill in personal details (name, age, address, etc.)
  3. Upload a photo (optional but recommended for face recognition)
  4. Enter criminal details (FIR number, case status, etc.)
  5. Add tags, associates, hideouts if available
  6. Click Save Record
📸 Photo Upload Tips:
  • Use clear, front-facing photos
  • Good lighting recommended
  • Avoid sunglasses or extreme angles
  • Photos are stored in your Firebase database (encrypted)

Searching Records

  • Text Search: Search by name, FIR number, address
  • Face Recognition: Click camera icon to scan a face and find matches
  • Filters: Filter by status, year, area, threat level
  • Advanced Filters: Combine multiple filters
💡 Face Recognition: Requires registered photos. Higher similarity threshold = better matches. Matches below 75% similarity may be unreliable.

Editing & Deleting Records

  • Click a record to open details
  • Click Edit to modify information
  • Click Delete to remove (⚠️ irreversible)

Officer Profiles

Viewing Your Profile

  1. Click Profile tab at bottom
  2. View your officer details (name, station, police ID)
  3. See your registered photo

Editing Your Profile

  1. Click Edit Profile button
  2. Update name or police station
  3. Upload a new photo by clicking on the image
  4. Click checkmark to save changes
⚠️ Profile Updates: Changes are saved to your Firebase account and synced across devices.

Secure Operations Room

The Operations Room is an encrypted P2P chat system for teams to share criminal records and coordinate investigations in real-time.

Joining an Operation

  1. Click Operations tab
  2. Enter Operation ID (e.g., ALPHA-1)
  3. Enter the Secret Passkey shared by your team
  4. Click Connect to Secure Channel

Sharing Records

  • In Operations Room, click + icon
  • Select a criminal record from your database
  • Record encrypted and shared with all connected peers

Chat Features

  • Send encrypted messages to team members
  • Edit or delete your messages
  • Share criminal records in-chat
  • Face scan to quickly find and share records
💡 Security: All operations use AES encryption. The passkey is encrypted and saved in Firebase automatically.
🔐 How Encryption Works:
  • Your passkey is hashed with your unique UID
  • Messages encrypted before leaving your device
  • Only peers with correct passkey can decrypt
  • No central server stores unencrypted data

Troubleshooting Connections

  • Timeout Error: Check internet connection, try again in 15 seconds
  • Room Offline: Operation creator must be online first, or create new room
  • Wrong Passkey: Messages will show encrypted if key mismatch

Bulk Data Management (Excel View)

Manage multiple records in a spreadsheet-like interface for efficient bulk operations.

Accessing Excel View

  1. Click Bulk Mgmt or Excel tab
  2. View all records in grid format
  3. Edit cells directly

Excel Operations

  • Add Row: Click + to add new record
  • Edit Cell: Click to edit, type, or use voice input (🎤)
  • Voice Input: Click microphone icon to speak data (speech-to-text)
  • Copy/Paste: Use Ctrl+C/V or right-click menu
  • Sort: Click column header to sort ascending/descending
  • Filter: Use search box to filter rows
  • Delete Row: Select row and click delete

Duplicate Detection

  • Automatically detects duplicate names, FIR numbers
  • Highlight duplicates or hide non-duplicates
  • Remove duplicates (keep first or last)

Undo/Redo

  • Undo: Ctrl+Z or click ↶ icon
  • Redo: Ctrl+Y or click ↷ icon
  • Undo stack: up to 40 steps

Analytics & Reporting

Dashboard Overview

Exporting Data

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes. All data is stored in your Firebase database (which you control), encrypted in transit, and encrypted at rest in operations room. GOAT'ECH cannot access your data.

Q: What if I lose my phone?

A: Your data is in Firebase, not on the phone. Log in on a new device with your email. Local cache will be cleared.

Q: Can I use this offline?

A: Yes, for viewing and editing. Sync happens when connection returns. Operations room requires internet.

Q: How do I share data with my team?

A: Use the Operations Room feature. Create an operation with a passkey and invite team members to join.

Q: What if I forget my password?

A: Click "Forgot Password" on login screen. Follow the email reset link from Firebase.

Q: How accurate is face recognition?

A: Requires high-quality registered photos. ~75% similarity is good confidence. Manual verification recommended before action.

Q: Can I delete my account?

A: Yes, go to Profile > Danger Zone > Delete My Account. This is permanent and removes all your data.
